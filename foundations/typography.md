# Typography

Type scale, families, weights, and usage rules for Shapes.

## Principles

- Type must be readable at the smallest sizes used in the system.
- Line length, leading, and contrast are not aesthetic choices. They affect comprehension.
- No decorative typefaces. Every font choice must survive a low-literacy reading context.
