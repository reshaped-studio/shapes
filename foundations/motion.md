# Motion

Motion principles and duration tokens for Shapes.

## Principles

- Motion must serve a purpose. It communicates state change, spatial relationship, or feedback. It does not decorate.
- All motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. When this preference is set, motion is removed or reduced to a simple fade at most.
- Shorter is better. Default to the shortest duration that still communicates the transition clearly.
